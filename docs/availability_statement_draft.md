# Availability statement draft

Public single-cell RNA-seq data are available from NCBI GEO under accession GSE191067, and the postoperative-recurrence bulk RNA-seq data are available from NCBI SRA under BioProject PRJNA813172. Analysis code, frozen gene definitions, parameter records, and machine-readable result tables are available at <https://github.com/y504071842/keloid-fibroblast-trajectory> and are archived in Zenodo at <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21503331>. Discovery-cohort sequencing data and processed objects are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to institutional ethics approval and applicable participant-consent restrictions.

The DOI above is reserved and becomes registered when the Zenodo record is published.
